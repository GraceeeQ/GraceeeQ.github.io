I am Yuhang Qiu (邱雨航), an undergraduate student in Computer Science at [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/), and an undergraduate researcher at [X-LANCE Lab](https://x-lance.sjtu.edu.cn/) advised by Prof. Kai Yu. I am also a member of the ACM Honors Class.

My research interests are in **Speech AI**, with a focus on speech understanding and generation. I am particularly interested in reliable automatic speech recognition, Large Audio Language Models, and ASR-TTS co-evolutionary systems.

I am currently working on ASR-TTS collaborative training and reliability-oriented ASR evaluation. Earlier, I contributed to ISA-Bench, a benchmark for instruction sensitivity in Large Audio Language Models.
